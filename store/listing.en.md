## Detailed description
```
You can't control yourself, but you can control your environment.

Pavio is a focus extension for people who get distracted easily. Instead of only blocking sites, you build environments: each one decides, site by site, what is allowed, blocked or restricted. When a session starts, a candle lights up and melts down as time passes. When it burns out, you get a notification.

• Routine by site and time — see your 24 hours as a timeline per site and draw when each one is blocked or has parts hidden: work hours, nights, weekends, or always. It works by itself.
• Quick block — block a whole app (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok…) with one click, and one more to release it. Nothing is blocked by default: the routine starts clean and you choose.
• Candle timer — for extra focus, start a 25/50/90-minute (or custom) session; the candle visibly melts and the rules of the environment you pick apply on top of your routine.
• Automatic Pomodoro — focus/break cycles for the timer; the timer's blocks switch off during breaks.
• Intent mode — on restricted sites, Pavio asks "what are you here to do?" and for how long, then nudges you gently when time is up.
• Friction to quit early — a message, a 10-second wait and a phrase to type (or a light confirmation mode). Loosening a rule that is protecting you right now asks for confirmation too.

HIDE PARTS, NOT THE WHOLE SITE
YouTube (feed, Shorts, recommendations, comments; optional allow-list of videos/playlists/channels), Instagram (feed, Reels, Explore, Stories), X ("For you" tab, Explore), LinkedIn (feed, videos, news).

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Optional scented-candle suggestions use affiliate links (Pavio may earn a commission; you can turn them off). Everything stays in your browser. Site access permissions are optional and requested per site.

Pavio is a focus aid, not a medical device. YouTube, Instagram, X and LinkedIn are trademarks of their owners; Pavio is independent and not affiliated with them. The interface is in Brazilian Portuguese.
```
